First public release of Kestrel, a Minecraft 1.8.9 PvP client.

- One self-contained Kestrel.exe: installs Minecraft 1.8.9 + Forge and the Kestrel client mod
- HUD, custom crosshair, toggle sprint, capes, skies, kill effects, performance presets
- Cosmetics editor in the launcher
- Automatic updates: signed releases are offered in the launcher and installed with one click
