Fixes launcher flicker and desktop stutter.

- The launcher now draws with OpenGL on Windows by default. On some NVIDIA / Windows 10 systems the previous Direct3D 12 renderer made the window flash washed-out frames and the whole desktop stutter while you moved the mouse over it or dragged it
- New Settings → Advanced → Renderer: Automatic, OpenGL or Direct3D 12 (takes effect on the next start)
- Settings are now saved one second after you change them (previously they could stay unsaved until the launcher closed)
- Your settings, accounts, game files, mods, resource packs and cosmetics are kept
