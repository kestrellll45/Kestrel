New QoL tab and Microsoft sign-in fixes.

- QoL > Mod Manager: see your Forge 1.8.9 mods with name, version and compatibility; install from a file, enable/disable, and remove (recoverable from "Recently removed")
- QoL > Resource Packs: preview, enable/disable and reorder packs; saved to Minecraft's own settings; Open Resource Pack Folder
- QoL > Fullbright: one switch for full brightness in caves and at night; applies instantly, even while playing, and never changes your Brightness setting
- Sign-in: Settings > Advanced now checks the Microsoft app (client) ID and explains what is wrong; sign-in pages are restricted to Microsoft's own sites
