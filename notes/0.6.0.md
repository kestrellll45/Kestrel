A big cosmetics update: 37 new capes, 26 new skies and 16 new kill effects.

- **Capes:** 24 new static designs (Kestrel Classic/Gold/Stealth, gradients, neon, geometric, elemental, pixel-art, Galaxy, Obsidian Rune, Royal Crest, Carbon) and 13 animated ones (RGB Cycle, RGB Wave, Plasma, Tidal Waves, Wildfire, Energy Core, Code Rain, Starfall, Pulse Rings, Kaleidoscope, Aurora Flow, Lava Lamp, Kestrel Pulse). Animated capes are marked ANIMATED and play in their previews
- **Skies:** 26 new skies in 9 categories: sunsets, space, clouds and aurora, dark, neon, fantasy, minimal, blocky and Kestrel. Pick "Vanilla sky" to go back to the normal sky. Switching takes effect immediately, with no restart
- **Kill effects:** 16 new effects (smoke bomb, pixel blast, electric shock, ice shatter, nether blaze, tornado, soul release, ender vanish, portal, dragon breath, energy burst, fireworks, confetti, rainbow rings, falling stars, Kestrel strike), each with its own sound. In game, clicking an effect equips it and plays it in front of you
- **Kill effects now only play for your own kills of other players:** never for mobs, other players' kills, fall/lava/void deaths or your own death. If it isn't clear that you got the kill, nothing plays. The "Include mobs" option is gone
- New menus in game (Right Shift) and in the launcher: category filters, a search box, a preview for every cape, sky and effect, and a clear EQUIPPED marker
- Your uploaded capes and skies keep working and are listed under Custom. Your settings, accounts, game files, mods, resource packs and cosmetics are kept
