Historical Mojang capes, smoother zoom and an instant free-look release.

- **Mojang capes:** a new Mojang category with 12 historical capes: MINECON 2011, 2012, 2013, 2015 and 2016, Realms MapMaker, Crafter, Translator, Mojang (Classic), Mojang (2015), Cobalt and Mojira Moderator. They use the original textures, downloaded from Mojang's texture server the first time you preview or equip one and then kept on your PC
- These are local Kestrel cosmetics: only your Kestrel client shows them. Equipping one does not give your account that cape, and other players don't see it
- **Zoom (C):** zooming in and out now eases in and out smoothly over a set time (Zoom speed), at any FOV. Letting go mid-zoom reverses from where it is, with no jump
- **Free-look (Left Alt):** the camera now returns the moment you let go. If Kestrel switched you to third person it goes straight back to first person; otherwise it eases back in about a tenth of a second
- Your settings, accounts, game files, mods, resource packs, capes, skies and kill effects are kept
