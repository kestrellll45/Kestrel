Zoom, 360° free-look and a local skin changer.

- **Zoom:** hold C to zoom in smoothly; let go to zoom back out. Your FOV setting is never changed. Set the strength and speed in the Kestrel menu (Right Shift → General)
- **Free-look:** hold Left Alt to look around 360° while you keep moving the way you were facing. The camera eases back when you let go. Camera-only: nothing changes for the server
- Rebind both under Options → Controls → Kestrel. Opening a menu, Alt-Tab, dying or changing world always releases them
- **Skin changer:** wear any skin in your own Kestrel client, from the new Skin page in the launcher or the Skin button on the Minecraft main menu. Import a PNG (64x64 or 64x32) or paste a NameMC profile link (namemc.com/profile/<name>). Choose classic or slim arms, or restore your account skin at any time
- Skins are local only: nothing is uploaded to your Microsoft/Mojang account, and other players keep seeing your real skin. NameMC skin pages (namemc.com/skin/…) can't be loaded automatically; download the PNG from the page and import it
- Pasting now works in the launcher's text fields
- Your settings, accounts, game files, mods, resource packs, capes and cosmetics are kept
